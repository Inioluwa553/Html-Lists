Ingredients for Jollof Rice

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Red Bell Pepper
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Salt
  • Seasoning Cubes

Steps to Prepare Jollof Rice

Step 1: Prepare the Ingredients
Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside. Blend the tomatoes, onions, and red bell pepper to create a smooth paste.
Step 2: Cook the Jollof Rice
Heat vegetable oil in a pot, add the blended tomato mixture, and cook until the oil separates. Add the washed rice, salt, and seasoning cubes. Pour in water and let it cook on low heat until the rice is done.
Step 3: Serve
Once the rice is cooked, remove it from the heat and let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
